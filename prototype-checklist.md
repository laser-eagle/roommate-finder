2.1 Welcome, Registration, and Login

 Welcome, sign up, and log in (UC-1.1, UC-1.2, F11): a welcome page with Sign up and Log in, a sign-up form where users pick one or more roles (renter, roommate seeker, landlord, property manager), and a login form.

2.3 Renter and Roommate Profiles

 References (F12): a section on a profile showing references from former roommates and landlords with who wrote them, plus a way to request or write one.

2.5 Business Profiles

 Business profile (F3): management company name, property portfolio, contact details, verified business information, and linked staff members.

2.6 Housing Search, Filters, and Map View

 Map and commute search (F31): results shown on a map, searching within a map area, and estimated travel time to a chosen place, alongside the existing filters.

2.9 Roommate Matching and Compatibility Scores

 Required vs. preferred criteria (F37): each search criterion marked Required or Preferred, with an importance level for preferred ones.

2.10 Favorites, Notes, and Listing Comparisons

 Listing comparison (F15): two to four listings side by side by total cost, features, availability, commute, and preference fit, with the best value in each row highlighted and sortable rows.

2.12 Messaging and Collaborative Housing Searches

 Collaborative shortlist (F28): a shortlist shared with roommate group members where each person can comment and vote on listings.

2.13 Rental Applications and Application Tracking

 Rental application (F17): a reusable form with personal information, income, rental history, references, and documents, plus choosing which listings to send it to, a joint group application, and a status list.
 Application review (F40): the landlord’s list of applications with statuses (Submitted, Under Review, Info Requested, Approved, Declined, Withdrawn), and actions to request info, leave team feedback, and send a decision.

2.14 Student, Identity, and Listing Verification

 Student verification (F24): verify through a campus email or third-party service, then show a verified-student badge on the profile.

2.15 Saved Searches and Daily Search Scheduling

 Saved searches (F23): a list of named saved searches for apartments, rooms, or roommates, each with its own criteria, an Edit option, and Run now.
 Daily schedule (F41): settings on a saved search for run time, time zone, pause/resume, and the next run time.
 Search history (F38): past runs with their matches, the last and next run times, and the delivery status of each run.
 New and updated listings (F45): results from a scheduled search labeled as new, price changed, or availability changed.

2.18 Household Planning and Move-in/Move-out

 Household agreement (F1): expectations, shared-space rules, chores, and responsibilities, which roommate group members can edit and share.
 Move-in/move-out checklist (F5): tasks, key handoffs, condition photos, meter readings, and important dates, with items that can be checked off and documents attached.

2.19 Shared Expenses and Rent Payments

 Shared expenses (F20): rent, utilities, and household costs split among roommates, showing each person’s share, balances, recorded payments, and a send-reminder button.
 Rent payments (F46): charges, receipts, balances, partial payments, and reminders, linked to the lease.

2.20 Resident Services and Document Sharing

 Maintenance request (F21): the resident submits a request with photos and an urgency level. The manager assigns staff, schedules the repair, and posts updates and building announcements.
 Document sharing (F22): stored documents listing who can see each one, an expiration date, and a revoke-access button.

2.21 Landlord and Property Management

 Team roles (F29): a staff list with roles (Leasing Agent, Maintenance, Accountant, Office Admin) and permissions for properties, messages, applications, resident records, and financial information.

2.22 Lease and Renewal Management

 Lease and renewal (F44): lease details and dates, signing through an e-signature service, a renewal offer sent 90 days before the lease ends, and deadline reminders.

2.23 Privacy, Security, and Moderation

 Report and moderation (F33): a report dialog (scam, misleading listing, harassment, spam), an admin review queue, and an appeal option.

2.24 Administrative Tools and Analytics

 Admin analytics (F27): listing performance, response times, successful matches, automated-search failures, and notification delivery.
